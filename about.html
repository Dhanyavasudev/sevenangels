ABOUT OUR MARITIME EXPERTISE

Seven Angels Shipping LLC is a specialist maritime services company based in Dubai,its team of employee have 15 years average of experience in marine field. This team decided to build a new company named Seven Angels Shipping. Working between main supplier and clients as expert coordinator to support marine fields and end-to-end support for ship owners, ship management, manning agents, offshore corporations and maritime professionals across the globe.

From Vessel Coordination and offshore structuring to marine surveying and technical engineering, we partner with clients at every stage of their maritime journey.

Who We Are

Seven Angels Shipping LLC supports stakeholders across the maritime and offshore ecosystem: ship builders, owners, agents, charterers, seafarers, offshore operators, underwriters and maritime law professionals. We streamline complex regulatory, technical and operational requirements into clear, manageable processes.

Operating from Dubai, we remain in close coordination with multiple flag states and classification societies, ensuring support that vessels, corporations & seafarers remain compliant with IMO conventions, flag requirements and international maritime standards. Our approach combines domain experience, disciplined documentation practices and client-centric coordination.

Our Journey

Built on Experience & Maritime Trust

Seven Angels Shipping LLC has evolved as a partner of choice for owners, operators and offshore entities that require reliable support in paperwork, compliance and technical coordination.

Instead of isolated services, we bring an integrated ecosystem perspective – connecting regulatory requirements, operational realities and commercial objectives.

Phase 01
Foundation in Maritime Documentation & Liaison

Started with a strong focus on vessel documentation, regulatory liaison and coordination with flag administrations and survey bodies.

Phase 02
Expansion into Offshore Corporations & Crew Support

Extended services to include offshore corporate setup, compliance structuring and seafarer documentation – enabling smoother operations for ship owners and managers.

Phase 03
Integrated Marine Survey & Engineering Support

Brought specialized partners and technical consultants to assist in surveys, inspections and engineering evaluations for a wide range of vessels and operations.

Phase 04
Global Clientele & Multi-Flag Coordination

Today, Seven Angels Shipping LLC supports clients across multiple jurisdictions and regions, coordinating with international stakeholders in a structured, time-bound manner.
